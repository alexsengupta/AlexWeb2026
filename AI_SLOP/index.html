About this Blog

The AI Slop blog provides links and summaries for AI-related articles that I found thought provoking. It's also a means to see the quality (and evolving quality) of so called AI Slop. The main post is generated by Claude.ai — the first part should be a summary of the original article and the second part Claude's critique of the article. Feel free to add your thoughts after the post.
