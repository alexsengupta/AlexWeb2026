What the model leaves out

It is a single well-mixed box. There is no spatial structure across the reef flat, no waves or wind, no distinction between corals, algae and sediment, no nutrients and no seasonal cycle. Photosynthesis, respiration, calcification and dissolution are prescribed rates rather than a community that grows and changes. The purpose is to show how the processes compete, not to reproduce a particular reef.

  • The same equations, with every parameter exposed, are in the model app.
  • Sanity checks worth running there: set the tidal range to zero, or set photosynthesis and calcification to zero, and confirm the lagoon tracks the ocean.
