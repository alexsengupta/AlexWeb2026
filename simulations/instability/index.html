How to Use

Panels

  • Channel View (top): Shows the x-y channel coloured by zonal velocity U(y), or by vorticity ζ during simulation. White arrows show the flow direction.
  • Velocity Profile (bottom-left): Line plot of U(y) vs y. During simulation, shows the evolving zonal-mean flow (orange) vs the initial profile (cyan).
  • PV Gradient (bottom-right): Plot of β − U″(y). Red shading marks where qy < 0.

Controls

  • Profile Type: Bickley (sech²), Gaussian, cosine, or linear shear.
  • U₀: Maximum jet speed.
  • Jet Width / Position: Shape of the jet.
  • β: Planetary vorticity gradient. Set to 0 for an f-plane.

Running Simulations

  • Click Run to integrate the barotropic vorticity equation forward in time.
  • The channel switches to showing the full 2D vorticity field.
  • Watch for meanders and eddy formation — that is barotropic instability.
  • Eddy KE / Total KE growing indicates energy transfer from the mean to eddies.
  • Perturbation Amplitude: Controls the initial noise added to seed instability.
  • Speed: Number of time steps per animation frame.

Suggested Experiments

  • Default Bickley jet — run it, watch meanders grow.
  • Linear shear — run it, confirm it stays stable.
  • Increase β until the Bickley jet stabilises, then run to verify.
  • Try β = 0 — does the jet still go unstable?
  • Narrow vs wide jet — how does width affect growth rate?

Physics Background

The Barotropic Vorticity Equation

The non-divergent barotropic vorticity equation on a β-plane is:

∂ζ/∂t + J(ψ, ζ + βy) = ν∇⁴ζ

where ζ = ∇²ψ is relative vorticity, ψ is the streamfunction, and ν∇⁴ζ is hyperdiffusion for numerical stability.

Basic State

We consider a zonal flow U(y) in a channel with rigid walls at y = 0 and y = L, periodic in x. The velocity is purely zonal: u = U(y), v = 0.

Rayleigh-Kuo Necessary Condition

Linearising about U(y) and seeking wave solutions ψ' ~ φ(y) eik(x-ct) gives the Rayleigh-Kuo equation:

(U - c)(φ'' - k²φ) + (β - U'')φ = 0

A necessary condition for instability (Im(c) > 0) is that the meridional PV gradient:

qy = β - U''(y)

must change sign somewhere within the domain.

Numerical Method

The simulation uses:

  • Arakawa Jacobian for advection (conserves energy and enstrophy)
  • Spectral Poisson solver (FFT in x, tridiagonal in y)
  • Adams-Bashforth 3 time stepping
  • Hyperdiffusion (ν∇⁴) for small-scale dissipation
  • Grid: 256×128, periodic in x, rigid walls in y (free-slip)

Velocity Profiles

  • Bickley jet: U = U₀ sech²((y - y₀)/Ljet)
  • Gaussian: U = U₀ exp(-(y - y₀)² / 2σ²)
  • Cosine: U = U₀ cos²(π(y - y₀) / 2Ljet) for |y - y₀| < Ljet
  • Linear: U = U₀(y/L - 0.5) — uniform shear, U'' = 0

Physical Interpretation

Barotropic instability extracts kinetic energy from the mean shear flow to grow wave perturbations. It is relevant to atmospheric jets, ocean western boundary currents, and planetary atmospheres.
